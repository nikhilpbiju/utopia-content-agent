Warehouse management systems don't talk to transport providers, transport

Every handoff is a phone call or a WhatsApp message.

[09:16] Sara: How are companies handling it today?

[09:17] Ahmed: Manual reconciliation. I talked to a warehouse operator last

[09:19] Sara: And your solution is the integration layer between those systems?

[09:20] Ahmed: Exactly. We're building an API-first middleware that sits
